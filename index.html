About Momma's Craft Cottage

Momma's Crafting Cottage mission is to provide a cozy, creative space where you and your friends can gather to craft and preserve memories which are timeless treasures of the heart. We aim to inpsire heartfelt storytelling through your personalized scrapbooks. Your retreat will help you capture and celebrate life's special moments, creating lasting keepsakes that can be treasured for generations.

Momma's Craft Cottage is a family owned and operated business. We are located in the heart of Brown County, Indiana. Our retreat is inspired by our mothers and the love they displayed for their families. We are excited to share our love of crafting with you and your friends. We hope you will join us for a retreat soon!
